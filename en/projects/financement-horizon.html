Horizon: financing the first R&D phase

I built the financing case and engaged BDC and PME MTL to secure $500,000 for Horizon, linking borrowed funds, R&D investment and the planned reinvestment of tax credits.

My role
Financing structure and leadership
Tools & areas
Financing · Financial modelling · R&D

The starting point

Horizon is a multi-year research and development project at SmartPixel. It aims to modernize the company’s technology foundations through a new CMS, a shared environment for its applications and a more data-driven approach.

The technical goal is to make code changes, new features, interventions and application maintenance easier. The project also includes redesigning the client-facing CMS, with an improved interface and user experience.

A significant part of Horizon also involves SmartPixel Web: a browser-based 3D real estate presentation and sales tool that requires no installation. It allows sales teams, brokers and buyers to explore a development remotely through a simple link.

To launch the first phase, SmartPixel needed to fund development resources while aligning the investment with its financial plans.

The goal

Secure $500,000 in financing to support this first phase and recruit a development team.

My objective was to make this multi-year project clear to financing partners: explain the needs, break down costs, present the outlook and demonstrate how the investment fitted into the company’s budgets.

The financing structure also needed to connect borrowed funds with tax credits on eligible R&D expenditures to support the project’s continuation.

The approach

Building the complete financing case

I built the entire dossier, from the project narrative to the materials presented to partners. I approached BDC and PME MTL and generated their interest in a financing structure for Horizon.

I presented and explained the results of the previous two financial years alongside the available preliminary results, connecting them to the project’s needs and outlook.

Modelling needs and scenarios

I broke down costs and prepared projections of how the financing and project would affect our budgets and revenue. I developed several scenarios to inform discussions with partners and internal decisions.

This work brought together the resources required, the development timeline and the financial assumptions in a coherent presentation.

Creating a reinvestment mechanism

I incorporated the relationship between borrowing and R&D tax credits into the financing structure: borrowed funds enable development expenditures, and the eligible portion may generate tax credits intended for reinvestment in the project.

This approach connects the initial financing to the continued funding needs of a multi-year R&D program.

Results & next steps

The financing structure secured $500,000 for Horizon’s first phase. These funds supported the recruitment of a development team to move the project forward.

My contribution was to turn a technology ambition into a financeable case: structure the needs, explain the financial results, model several scenarios and present a coherent rationale to partners.

The “Completed” status refers to securing this financing. The next steps concern Horizon’s continued development and the planned reinvestment of R&D tax credits, subject to eligible expenditures. This first phase is one step in the program, rather than completion of the entire project.
